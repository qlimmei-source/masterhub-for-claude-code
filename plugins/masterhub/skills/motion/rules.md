# Rules for scene, look and movement

The bar is an Apple launch video: a frame that cannot stand next to it is not ready.

## Scene
- 3-6 seconds, one idea, one main object on 60-70 % of the frame, one "before, after" change.
- The transition is made by an object in the frame: it moves, grows, turns into the next scene. No template wipes or fades.
- Numbers roll like an odometer; charts flow instead of blinking; the camera pushes into an element and it becomes the scene.
- One peak moment per video: build toward it and do not spend it early.
- Motion shows, it does not write. Three levels: text, the real content of the subject (a screenshot, a live interface), motion that shows the subject itself. Always aim for the third (after a video by Igor Brekhov).

## Look
- Depth: several soft shadows, glass with a thin light edge.
- One or two accent colors; the accent only on what to look at now.
- One font, bold, tight tracking.
- The main word or number from 120 px, captions from 34 px, up to five words in a frame, margins from 60 px.
- Brand colors and fonts only as they are visible on the site or a screenshot. Do not guess.
- 30-40 % of the frame is empty space (after a video by Igor Brekhov).

## Movement
- Springs with little settling; linear only for rotation and drift.
- An entrance eases out softly, an exit accelerates and is half as long as the entrance.
- Words with a step of about 0.09 s, list items about 0.2 s. Never all at once, and never with the same delay for everything.
- The result of a step stays from 1.2 s, the final thought from 2 s.
- Light grain and a slowly drifting background; one focus.

### Movement numbers in frames (after a video by Igor Brekhov, at 30 frames per second)
| What | How much | How |
|---|---|---|
| Element enters | 10-18 frames (0.33-0.6 s) | soft landing, slowing down at the end |
| Element leaves | 8-12 frames (0.27-0.4 s) | accelerates, leaves past the frame edge |
| Pause between words | 4-5 frames (about 0.15 s) | for a fast row; for a calm one, the 0.09 s step from the rules above |
| Motion blur | on fast moves | several sub-frames per frame, only on short scenes |
| Event on screen | on the frame where the word is spoken | in a video with speech: the animation action lands exactly on its word |

The numbers are a guide. If the eye sees heaviness or a jerk, adjust by frames.

## If the video has a person speaking to the camera
- You need a transcript with the time of every word; put the animation action on its word, accuracy 0.15 s.
- Graphics not on the face. Camera angles change every 4-8 s, none longer than 12 s.
- Do not overwrite the source: work with a copy.
- Speech editing order and sound: `montage.md`.

## Not allowed
Centered text on a gradient; bounces, glow, particles, confetti, emoji, stock icons, clip art, gray placeholders; small objects in an empty frame; invented numbers, reviews, other companies' logos; an empty or black first frame.
